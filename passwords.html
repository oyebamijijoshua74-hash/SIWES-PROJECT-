Theory
Why Passphrases Beat Short Passwords
A password like P@$$w0rd! looks complex but is only 9 chars and easily cracked by rule-based dictionary attacks in minutes. A random 4-word passphrase like correct-horse-battery-staple provides 60+ bits of entropy and takes centuries to brute-force — yet it's easier to remember.
E = L × log₂(R) — Shannon Entropy Formula
Best Practice
Three Golden Rules of Credential Defense
1
Unique Per Service. Never reuse your university portal or bank password elsewhere.
2
Use a Password Manager. Bitwarden or 1Password safely stores 100+ unique 20-character passwords.
3
Enable MFA. Even if a password leaks, Multi-Factor Authentication blocks unauthorized login.
