Attack Vectors

Common Phishing Techniques

Vector 1
Deceptive Sender Domains
Attackers display a name like "University Registrar" while the actual address is support@university-portal.xyz. Always expand the sender header and inspect the domain after the @ symbol.
Vector 2
Fabricated Urgency & Fear
Phrases like "Account suspended in 2 hours" or "Arrest warrant issued" provoke panic, causing victims to bypass rational verification. Legitimate institutions do not demand credentials over email.
Vector 3
Weaponized Attachments
Watch for unexpected invoices or receipts with dangerous extensions: .iso, .vbs, .hta, or double extensions like invoice.pdf.exe. Never open attachments from unexpected senders.
