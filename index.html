Core Principles
The 4 Pillars of Cyber Defense
01
Zero Trust Mindset. Never trust unsolicited messages or links by default. Always verify identity through an out-of-band channel.
02
Multi-Factor Everywhere. Layer access so stolen passwords alone cannot grant entry to your accounts.
03
Patch & Upgrade Promptly. Keep operating systems and software patched against known exploits.
04
3-2-1 Backups. Three copies, two media types, one offsite — the ransomware antidote.
